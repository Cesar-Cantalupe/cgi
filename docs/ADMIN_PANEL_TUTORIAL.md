# eduCGI — How to Edit Website Texts

This guide explains how to sign in to the admin panel and update the text shown on the eduCGI website.

---

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the admin panel URL provided by your team:
   ```
   https://your-domain.com/admin/login
   ```
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **"Entrar al panel"** (*Enter the panel*).

You will be taken to the text editor.

---

## 2. Choose a language

At the top of the screen, click the language you want to edit:

**English** · **Español** · **Français** · **Deutsch** · **Català** · **Ελληνικά**

Each language has its own texts. If you need the same change in several languages, repeat the steps for each one.

---

## 3. Choose a section

On the left side, click the area of the website you want to edit:

| Section | What it contains |
|---------|------------------|
| **Cabecera** (Header) | Top bar text |
| **Pie de página** (Footer) | Footer links, copyright, grant information |
| **Inicio** (Home) | Welcome page — title, descriptions, START button |
| **Bienvenida** (Welcome) | Chat greeting and welcome messages |
| **Sugerencias** (Suggested questions) | Predefined question buttons on the welcome screen |
| **Barra lateral** (Sidebar) | Side menu and chat history labels |
| **Fuentes** (Sources) | Source and reference labels |
| **Feedback** | Like, dislike, and feedback form text |
| **Legal** | Legal page titles and content |

On a phone or small screen, use the dropdown menu instead of the left sidebar.

---

## 4. Find and edit a text

1. Scroll through the list, or use the search box (**"Buscar clave o texto…"**) to find a specific text.
2. Click inside the field and type your new text.
3. Changed fields are highlighted in amber.

To undo a change before saving, click **"Deshacer"** (*Undo*) on that field.

---

## 5. Save your changes

1. When you are done, click **"Guardar en Firebase"** (*Save to Firebase*) at the top right.
2. Wait until a green confirmation message appears.
3. Open the public website, select the language you edited, and **reload the page** to see your changes.

> **Important:** Changes are not saved automatically. Always click **"Guardar en Firebase"** before closing the panel or leaving the page.

---

## 6. Sign out

Click **"Salir"** (*Exit*) at the top right when you are finished.

If you have unsaved changes, the system will ask you to confirm before leaving.

---

## Quick tips

- **Save often** — click **"Guardar en Firebase"** regularly.
- **Do not switch language** with unsaved changes unless you are sure — you may lose your edits.
- **Reload the public site** after saving to verify your changes.
- If you cannot log in or something does not work, contact your administrator.
