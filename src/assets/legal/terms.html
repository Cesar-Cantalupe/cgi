Project

eduCGI is an informative web‑based AI chatbot designed to help users understand complex genomic concepts in clear and accessible language. It employs controlled Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG) mechanisms and an open‑source large language model (LLM) to provide responses derived exclusively from pre‑validated and evidence‑based sources. The tool does not require user registration and does not process or store personal data, as any personal data voluntarily or accidentally entered by Users is automatically detected and filtered out.
